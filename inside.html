Save Hours: AI Shortcuts for Contractors. Five shortcuts for estimates, follow-ups, job updates, review requests, and a kitchen rendering. Written in plain English.

Five shortcuts

  1. 01

    Start here

    Cabinet color, from one photo

    Photograph the kitchen. Ask ChatGPT for a precise image-edit prompt that keeps the layout and the camera angle, then change only the cabinet color in an AI image tool. The steps in the guide are the ones Ryan Newland uses. He is a licensed general contractor with Newland General Construction in North County San Diego.

  2. 02

    Job notes into estimate scope

    Turn the notes from a walkthrough into a scope you can price: what the job includes, what it does not, and what is still open.

  3. 03

    Bid follow-up messages

    A clear follow-up you can send after the bid goes out, specific to that job, so the next note is not waiting on your memory at the end of the day.

  4. 04

    Daily job updates from voice notes

    Say what happened on site. Get a client update in plain language, ready to send.

  5. 05

    Review requests

    Ask for a review while the work is still fresh, in a note that sounds like a person.
