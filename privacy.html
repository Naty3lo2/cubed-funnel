The short version: When you ask for the free guide, I collect your first name, email, and trade. I use them to send you the guide and a few follow-up emails. I never sell your information, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

When you fill out the free guide form, we collect:

We never sell, rent, or trade your information. We don't share it with other companies for their marketing.

Every follow-up email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email natalyvidales@gmail.com at any time to unsubscribe, ask what information we have about you, or ask us to delete it. We'll take care of it promptly.

This page does not use advertising cookies. If we add basic visit counting later, we'll update this policy.
